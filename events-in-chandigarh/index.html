Events in Chandigarh

A city that shows up together.

The festivals, fairs and shows Chandigarh comes back to every year, and where to look for what’s on this week.

Through the year

The annual calendar.

These events come round every year, but exact dates and venues change. Timings below are what organisers usually announce, so confirm with them before you go.

  • Feb – Mar

    Rose Festival

    Also called the Festival of Gardens: a three-day celebration at the Zakir Hussain Rose Garden, Sector 16. Recent editions have included music nights and stalls.

    Chandigarh Tourism (opens in a new tab)

  • Monsoon

    Teej at the Rock Garden

    For Teej, the monsoon festival, Nek Chand’s Rock Garden takes on a festive look. The date follows the lunar calendar and usually falls in July or August.

    Chandigarh Tourism (opens in a new tab)

  • Early Oct

    Chandigarh National Craft Mela

    A national crafts fair at Kalagram with traditional handicrafts, handlooms and artisans from across India. Usually held in the first or second week of October.

    Chandigarh Tourism (opens in a new tab)

  • Nov – Dec

    Military Literature Festival

    Panel discussions and military exhibitions, held every year since 2017 and organised by the Punjab Government, the Chandigarh Administration and the Army’s Western Command. Recent editions have been at the Lake Sports Complex, Sukhna Lake.

    Festival website (opens in a new tab)

  • Late Nov

    Chandigarh Carnival

    A city-wide carnival with parades, performances, food and family activities at Leisure Valley, Sector 10. Usually held on the last weekend of November.

    Chandigarh Tourism (opens in a new tab)

  • December

    Chrysanthemum Show

    A seasonal flower show of chrysanthemum varieties and gardening skill, traditionally at the Terraced Garden, Sector 33. Check the venue each year.

    Chandigarh Tourism (opens in a new tab)

  • Late Dec

    New Year Extravaganza

    Chandigarh Tourism’s year-end celebrations, held in the last week of December.

    Chandigarh Tourism (opens in a new tab)

Any week of the year

Where the shows are.

For plays, concerts and dance between the big festivals, check these stages’ own listings, or call the Chandigarh Tourism helpline on 1800 180 2116

  • Sector 18

    Tagore Theatre

    The city’s main stage for plays, classical dance and music concerts.

  • Sector 16

    Punjab Arts Council

    Folk arts, poetry recitals and cultural showcases.

  • Sector 23-B

    Bal Bhawan

    Navrang Theatre and an open-air theatre, both used for plays, dance and concerts.

  • Sector 17

    Plaza Stage

    Street shows, music and festive events in the city centre’s pedestrian plaza.

  • Sector 1

    Rock Garden Amphitheatre

    Occasional open-air concerts and cultural nights among Nek Chand’s sculptures.
