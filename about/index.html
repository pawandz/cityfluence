About us

Built for our city. Powered by its people.

Our story

Chandigarh has its own rhythm — its cafés, creators, communities, entrepreneurs and distinctive way of life.

Chandigarhcitylyf exists to bring those worlds closer together. We help local businesses discover creative talent, give creators meaningful collaboration opportunities, and tell stories that celebrate the people and places shaping the Tricity.

Our ambition is simple: make local discovery more meaningful and local collaborations more accessible.

Our mission

Connect the city’s creative and commercial life.

Make it simple for local businesses and creators to find each other, and for audiences to discover what’s worth their time.

Why Chandigarh

A city with a strong identity.

A planned city with a distinctive culture, a young population and a Tricity that lives as one — the right scale for meaningful local connections.

What we believe

  • Local beats generic.
  • Fit matters more than follower count.
  • Honest numbers or no numbers.
  • Paid content is always labelled.
  • Creators own their work and their data.

How we help creators

A professional home for your work.

A profile you approve, relevant briefs, clear terms and fair, documented collaborations.

How we help businesses

The right creators, with a plan.

Objective-led campaigns, creator matching, coordination and honest reporting based on the data available.

Our editorial approach

Independent, verified, labelled.

We verify addresses, dates and details before publishing, credit sources and photographers, and clearly separate editorial recommendations from sponsored features.
