Ad formats

Ways to reach the Tricity.

Each format runs on Chandigarhcitylyf’s own pages and channels. Looking for creator campaigns instead? See For Businesses

  • 01

    Sponsored stories

    An article about your business, product or event in Stories & Guides, written to our editorial standard and clearly labelled as sponsored.

  • 02

    Featured placements

    A highlighted spot in City Life and in our guides to cafés, food, fashion and nightlife.

  • 03

    Event promotion

    Listings and features for openings, pop-ups, workshops and gigs, timed to run before the date.

  • 04

    Series sponsorship

    Present a guide or a recurring series, for example a seasonal food trail, with your brand credited throughout.

  • 05

    Social posts & reels

    Sponsored posts on Chandigarhcitylyf’s own social handles once they launch. Ask us about early slots.

  • 06

    Custom partnerships

    Got an idea that doesn’t fit a box? Tell us. We’ll work out a format that suits your brand and our readers.

Our standards

Advertising readers can trust.

  • Every paid placement is clearly labelled as sponsored or advertising.
  • Sponsored content is kept separate from our independent stories and guides.
  • We only take on businesses and offers we’re comfortable putting in front of our readers.
  • We share the performance data we have, and we never promise guaranteed reach, footfall or sales.

Rates depend on the format, duration, placement and any production involved. We’ll send a quote once we understand your brief.
