How this list was made

We started from Pantheon’s Chandigarh page (23 people) and extended it with a Wikidata search for everyone recorded as born in Chandigarh with an English Wikipedia article, ranked by how widely they are covered. Someone appears under “Born in Chandigarh” only when Wikidata records it and their Wikipedia article supports it; where Wikipedia gives another birthplace but describes growing up here, they appear under “Raised in Chandigarh”. Every profile is written from the person’s public Wikipedia article, linked on their page, and was checked on 11 October 2026.
