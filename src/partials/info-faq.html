FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free for creators to join?

Yes. Registering is free and there is no exclusivity. Your profile goes live only after you approve what is shown.

How much does a campaign cost?

Every campaign is quoted individually. Final costs depend on creator fees, production requirements, content usage rights and campaign scope. Share your brief and we’ll come back with a proposal.

Do you guarantee followers, reach or sales?

No. We plan campaigns around clear objectives and review them honestly using the performance data that is actually available — but we never promise guaranteed results.

How are “Featured Creators” chosen?

It is an editorial selection by the Cityfluence team, not a paid ranking or a score. Audience numbers are shown only when they come from a verified, authorised source.

Can my business be featured?

Yes — apply through the business inquiry form or call us. Features are written with your permission and verified details. Paid features are always labelled “Sponsored”.

Which areas do you cover?

Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula. If you’re elsewhere in the region, get in touch and we’ll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How do you handle my personal data?

We only use the details you share to respond to you. Read the Privacy Policy for more.
