Creators: How to Build a Media Kit Brands Actually Read

Most local businesses spend less than a minute on a creator’s media kit. Make that minute count with clarity rather than decoration.

By Chandigarhcitylyf Editorial Published 11 Oct 2026 Updated 11 Oct 2026 4 min read

Lead with who you are

Name, handle, niche and location in one line. Then a two-sentence description of the kind of content you make and who it is for.

Share real, recent numbers

Use screenshots or exports from your platform’s own insights, with the date they were taken. Include audience location if a large share is in the Tricity — local businesses care about that more than total reach.

Show three pieces of your best work

Pick work that matches the brand you are pitching. Link to the original posts so they can see the comments and context.

Make services and terms easy to understand

  • What you offer: reels, photo sets, stories, event coverage.
  • Typical turnaround times.
  • Whether usage rights are included or priced separately.
  • How brands should contact you.

A clear, honest one-pager beats a beautiful twenty-page deck.
