SonamLamba

Television actor

  • Born26 February 1997
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 2015breakthrough role

Sonam Lamba is a television actor best known as Vidya Modi in Saath Nibhaana Saathiya. She has since led Ek Duje Ki Parchai and Jahaan Chaand Rehta Hai.

Early roles

Born in Chandigarh, she first appeared as Ritu Pandey in Desh Ki Beti Nandini (2013–14), with roles in Balika Vadhu and Diya Aur Baati Hum.

Breakthrough and leads

Her role as Vidya in Saath Nibhaana Saathiya (2015–2017) made her known. She returned as the lead in Ek Duje Ki Parchai (2021), joined Sasural Simar Ka 2 in 2022 and led Jahaan Chaand Rehta Hai on DD National in 2023.
