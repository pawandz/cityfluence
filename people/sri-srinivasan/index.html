SriSrinivasan

Chief Judge, US Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit

  • Born23 February 1967
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 25cases argued before the US Supreme Court
  • 2020chief judge since

Sri Srinivasan is an American jurist who has served as chief judge of the United States Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit since 2020. He argued 25 cases before the US Supreme Court and was considered for a Supreme Court nomination in 2016.

Chandigarh to Kansas

Srinivasan was born in Chandigarh. His family settled in Lawrence, Kansas, in 1971, where his father taught mathematics at the University of Kansas. He studied at Stanford University and earned a joint JD–MBA from Stanford in 1995.

Supreme Court advocate to chief judge

As principal deputy solicitor general of the United States, he argued 25 cases before the Supreme Court. He was a partner at O’Melveny & Myers and lectured at Harvard Law School before joining the D.C. Circuit, becoming chief judge in 2020.
