VivekShauq

Actor, comedian, writer and singer

  • Born21 June 1963
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • Died10 January 2011
  • 2cinemas: Hindi and Punjabi

Vivek Shauq was an actor, comedian, writer and singer who worked across Hindi and Punjabi film, television and theatre. He came to prominence alongside Jaspal Bhatti in Doordarshan’s Flop Show and Ulta Pulta.

A Chandigarh comic voice

Born in Chandigarh, Vivek started in theatre and television. He was a founding member of the Nonsense Club and was regarded by Jaspal Bhatti as his right-hand man on shows including Ulta Pulta, Flop Show and Full Tension.

Films

His Hindi film debut was Barsaat Ki Raat (1998). He appeared in Gadar: Ek Prem Katha, Aitraaz, 36 China Town and Delhi Heights, and in Punjabi films including Asa Nu Maan Watna Da and Mini Punjab. He died in Mumbai on 10 January 2011, aged 47.
