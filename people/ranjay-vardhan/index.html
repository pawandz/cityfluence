RanjayVardhan

Sociologist and author

  • Born3 May 1969
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 15books
  • 30+research articles

Dr Ranjay Vardhan heads the Department of Sociology at the Post Graduate Government College for Girls, Sector 42. The author of 15 books, he is known for research on family, gender and broken hearts, and for the concept of “Covidalization”.

A Chandigarh education

He went to DAV Senior Secondary School in Sector 8, graduated from Post Graduate Government College, Sector 11, and earned his master’s and doctorate in sociology at Panjab University, where he also studied French, German and Persian.

Heartbreak and society

His work on broken hearts earned media nicknames such as “Newton of Heartbreak”. He has published 15 books and over 30 research articles, and was invited to present at Harvard University in 2012.
