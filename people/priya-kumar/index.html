PriyaKumar

Author and motivational speaker

  • Born4 March 1974
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 17books
  • 22age she began speaking

Priya Kumar is a motivational speaker and the author of 17 novels and self-help books on inspirational and spiritual themes. Her novel I Will Go with You (2015) was adapted into the web series The Final Call.

Chandigarh beginnings

Born in Chandigarh, she studied at St. Kabir Public School, graduated in economics from the University of Bombay, and began speaking professionally at 22.

Seventeen books

Her first book, License to Live (2010), was nominated for a Vodafone Crossword Book Award and won an Eric Hoffer Award in 2012. She has also written a biography of Hero Cycles founder Om Prakash Munjal. In 2019 her novel I Will Go with You became The Final Call, starring Arjun Rampal.
