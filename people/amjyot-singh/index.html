AmjyotSingh

Basketball player — NBA G League pioneer

  • Born27 January 1992
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 6′8″height
  • 1stIndians in the NBA G League (with Princepal Singh)

Amjyot Singh Gill is a 6 ft 8 in forward who played for India at several FIBA Asia Championships. He was among the first Indian men to sign a professional contract abroad, and one of the first two Indians in the NBA G League.

From cricket to court

Born in Chandigarh to an athlete mother and a father who played basketball for India, Amjyot started as a cricket bowler. An injury in school turned him to basketball in 2007; within a year he was in the Punjab and national under-16 teams, and he trained at the Ludhiana Basketball Academy.

Abroad and the G League

He signed with Japan’s Yokohama Excellence in 2015 and later played for the Oklahoma City Blue and Wisconsin Herd in the NBA G League.
