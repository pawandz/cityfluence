VivekDahiya

Actor (TV, film and streaming)

  • Born8 November 1984
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • S8Nach Baliye winner

Vivek Dahiya is a television, film and web-series actor. He won Nach Baliye 8 in 2017 and has appeared in Yeh Hai Mohabbatein, State of Siege: 26/11 and the film Chal Zindagi.

From Chandigarh to screen

Born in Chandigarh, Vivek earned a master’s in international business management at De Montfort University in England before acting.

Screens

He became known for Yeh Hai Mohabbatein and Qayamat Ki Raat, won Nach Baliye 8 in 2017, and has since led the film Chal Zindagi and appeared in State of Siege: 26/11 and State of Siege: Temple Attack.
