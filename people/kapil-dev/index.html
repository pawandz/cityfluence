Cricketer and 1983 World Cup–winning captain

Kapil Dev is widely regarded as one of the greatest all-rounders in cricket history — a fast-medium bowler and hard-hitting middle-order batter who led India to its first World Cup title in 1983, aged just 24.

Chandigarh roots

Kapil Dev Nikhanj was born in Chandigarh to Ram Lal Nikhanj, a teak merchant, and Rajkumari. He studied at D.A.V. College in the city.

A record-breaking career

He made a striking first-class debut for Haryana in 1975, taking six wickets against Punjab. At international level he became the only player to take more than 400 wickets (434) and score more than 5,000 runs in Test cricket, and he retired in 1994 as the first bowler to take 200 ODI wickets.

1983 and beyond

As captain he won the 1983 Cricket World Cup — still the youngest captain to lift the trophy. He was also part of the squad that won the 1985 World Championship of Cricket, and later coached the Indian team from 1999 to 2000.
