Taniya Bhatia is a wicket-keeper who plays for Punjab and India. The ICC named her one of five breakout stars in women’s cricket in 2018, and she has played in T20 World Cups, a 50-over World Cup and a Test.

A cricketing family

Her father, who works at the Central Bank of India, played university-level cricket. Taniya trained in Chandigarh under Yograj Singh — Yuvraj Singh’s father — while at DAV Senior Secondary School.

Record-young starts

She represented Punjab’s under-19 side at 11 and, at 13, became the youngest player for Punjab’s senior team in the inter-state tournament. In 2015 she captained the North Zone under-19 side.

India

She debuted in T20 Internationals against South Africa in February 2018, in ODIs that September, and in Tests against England in June 2021. She was part of squads for the 2018 and 2020 T20 World Cups, the 2022 World Cup and the 2022 Commonwealth Games.
