HarmanBaweja

Actor, producer and screenwriter

  • Born13 November 1980
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 3roles: actor, producer, writer
  • 2023comeback in Scoop

Harman Baweja is the son of director Harry Baweja and producer Pammi Baweja. He starred in Hindi films including Love Story 2050 and What’s Your Raashee?, returned in Netflix’s Scoop (2023), and writes and produces for film and streaming.

Born in Chandigarh

Harman was born in Chandigarh in 1980; his family moved to Mumbai in 1984. He trained at the Kishore Namit Kapoor Acting Institute and the University of California, Los Angeles, and spent around seven years assisting his father as a director.

On screen

He debuted in the sci-fi romance Love Story 2050 (2008), followed by Victory and What’s Your Raashee? (both 2009) and Dishkiyaoon (2014). After a long break he played JCP Harshavardhan Shroff in Hansal Mehta’s Netflix series Scoop.

Behind the camera

He has produced films including Punjabi titles and co-written series such as Super V and Bhaukaal.
