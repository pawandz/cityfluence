Former captain of Canada’s cricket team

  • Born10 October 1988
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 140off 94 balls vs Malaysia
  • CCanada captain

Navneet Dhaliwal is a former captain of the Canada national cricket team. Repeatedly Canada’s leading run-scorer in qualifying tournaments, he hit 140 off 94 balls against Malaysia in 2019.

Born in Chandigarh

Navneet was born in Chandigarh and moved to Canada at 22, when his family bought a gas station there.

Leading Canada

He debuted for Canada in 2015 and was its leading run-scorer at several 2018–19 tournaments. Named captain in 2019, he made his T20I debut against the Cayman Islands and scored 140 from 94 balls against Malaysia as Canada made 408.
