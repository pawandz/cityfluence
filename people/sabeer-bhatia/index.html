SabeerBhatia

Entrepreneur and Hotmail co-founder

  • Born30 December 1968
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 1996Hotmail launched
  • ~$400Mreported Microsoft acquisition price
  • 2years from launch to acquisition

Sabeer Bhatia is an Indian-American entrepreneur who co-founded Hotmail.com in 1996, the first free web-based email service. Microsoft acquired it in 1998 for an estimated $400 million.

From Chandigarh to Caltech

Born in Chandigarh, Sabeer studied in Pune and Bengaluru, then at BITS Pilani before earning a transfer scholarship to the California Institute of Technology. He completed a master’s in electrical engineering at Stanford University.

Hotmail

After working at Apple as a hardware engineer, he and colleague Jack Smith launched Hotmail on 4 July 1996 — a date chosen to symbolise freedom from ISP-based email. He led the company as president and CEO until Microsoft’s acquisition.

Next ventures

He later started the messaging service JaxtrSMS, proposed the “Nano City” project in Haryana, and in 2021 co-founded ShowReel.
