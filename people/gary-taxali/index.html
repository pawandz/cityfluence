GaryTaxali

Pop artist and illustrator

  • Born1968
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • OCADfull professor

Gary Taxali is a Canadian artist working across fine art and illustration in pop art, known for retro-inspired, cartoon-style work. His clients include The New York Times, TIME, Rolling Stone and The New Yorker.

Chandigarh to Toronto

Born in Chandigarh and raised in Toronto, he graduated from the Ontario College of Art in 1991 and is now a tenured full professor at OCAD University.

Style

His work mixes vintage comics, advertising and street art, using humour to explore consumer insecurities, relationships and isolation. He has served on Canada Post’s Stamp Advisory Committee.
