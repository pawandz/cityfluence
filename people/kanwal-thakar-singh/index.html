KanwalThakar Singh

Badminton champion and Arjuna awardee

  • Born2 November 1954
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 2national singles titles
  • 15age at her Punjab State title

Kanwal Thakar Singh was one of India’s leading badminton players of the late 1970s and early 1980s — a national singles champion, Commonwealth and Asian Games medallist, and Arjuna Award recipient.

A Chandigarh champion

Born in Chandigarh to an Army officer and a theatre actress, she began playing at 11 and won the Punjab State title at 15. She studied history at the Post Graduate Government College for Girls in the city.

National and international

She won national singles titles in 1977 and 1978, played the Uber Cup, won doubles bronze at the 1978 Commonwealth Games with Ami Ghia, reached the All England quarter-finals in 1979, and won bronze medals at the 1982 Asian Games in New Delhi.
