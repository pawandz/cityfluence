RandeepSurjewala

Politician and Rajya Sabha MP

  • Born3 June 1967
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 2022Rajya Sabha MP since

Randeep Singh Surjewala is an Indian National Congress politician from Haryana. A former Haryana minister — the youngest of his time — he became a party general secretary in 2021 and a Rajya Sabha MP in 2022.

Chandigarh and Panjab University

Born in Chandigarh, the youngest child of Haryana minister Shamsher Singh Surjewala, he earned a BCom (Hons), a law degree and a master’s in political science at Panjab University, Chandigarh.

Politics

He served as a Haryana MLA until 2019 and held portfolios including public works and IT. In 2005 he became the youngest minister in Haryana. He is a national spokesperson and general secretary of the Congress, and was elected to the Rajya Sabha from Rajasthan in 2022.
