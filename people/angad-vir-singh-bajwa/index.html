AngadVir Singh Bajwa

Skeet shooter and world-record holder

  • Born29 November 1995
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 60/60world-record skeet final
  • Tokyo2020 Olympian
  • 120/125Olympic qualification score

Angad Vir Singh Bajwa is a skeet shooter who, in 2018, became the first to shoot a perfect 60 out of 60 in a skeet final — India’s first gold in the discipline at a continental or world level. He represented India at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics and switched nationality to Canada in 2025.

Born in Chandigarh

Angad was born in Chandigarh, finished school at Sherwood College in 2013, and briefly studied at the University of British Columbia before returning to India to focus on shooting.

A perfect final

At the 2018 Asian Championships in Kuwait City he won gold with a world-record 60/60 in the final. At the 2019 Asian Championships in Doha he won gold after a shoot-off, earning a Tokyo 2020 quota place, where he finished 18th.

Team medals and a new flag

He won team gold at the 2021 World Cup in New Delhi and the 2023 Asian Championships, and team bronze at the 2022 Asian Games. In 2025 he changed his sporting nationality to Canada, where his family is based.
