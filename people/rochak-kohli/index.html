RochakKohli

Bollywood music director

  • Born3 July 1983
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • LLBPanjab University

Rochak Kohli is a Bollywood music director who debuted with “Pani Da Rang” in Vicky Donor and is known for “Paaniyon Sa” (Satyameva Jayate), “Tera Yaar Hoon Main” (Sonu Ke Titu Ki Sweety) and “Lae Dooba” (Aiyaary).

Lawyer turned composer

Born in Chandigarh, Rochak is a qualified lawyer with an LLB from Panjab University. He was national creative head of the radio station BIG FM 92.7 before turning to film music.

Film songs

His debut, “Pani Da Rang” in Shoojit Sircar’s Vicky Donor (2012), started a run that includes “Paaniyon Sa”, “Tera Yaar Hoon Main” and “Lae Dooba”, as well as the background score of Welcome 2 Karachi (2015).
