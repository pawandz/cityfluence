HitendraWadhwa

Columbia Business School professor and founder

  • Born25 January 1967
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • $10Mraised for Paramark

Hitendra Wadhwa is a professor at Columbia Business School and the founder of the Mentora Institute. Before academia he was a McKinsey consultant and a Silicon Valley founder.

Chandigarh to MIT

Born in Chandigarh, he studied mathematics at St. Stephen’s College, Delhi, then earned an MBA and a PhD in management science at MIT Sloan.

Consulting, start-ups and teaching

He joined McKinsey in 1996, raised $10 million to found the marketing-technology start-up Paramark in 1999 (acquired in 2002), taught at Wharton, and has been a Columbia Business School professor since 2006.
