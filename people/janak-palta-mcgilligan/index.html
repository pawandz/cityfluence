JanakPalta McGilligan

Social worker and Padma Shri recipient

  • Born1948
  • HometownChandigarh (raised)
  • 26years leading Barli

Janak Palta McGilligan is a Padma Shri–awarded social worker. She was founding director of the Barli Development Institute for Rural Women in Indore for 26 years and now leads the Jimmy McGilligan Centre for Sustainable Development.

A Chandigarh education

Growing up in Chandigarh, she earned an MA in English literature and political science, an MPhil with distinction and a PhD, along with the Sangeet Visharad in sitar, while working at the Provident Fund office, the High Court and the Centre for Rural and Industrial Development.

Barli and beyond

From its founding on 1 June 1985 until 2011 she directed the Barli Development Institute for Rural Women. She now directs the Jimmy McGilligan Centre for Sustainable Development, advises Solar Cookers International, and supports breast-cancer patients through Sangini Cancer Care.
