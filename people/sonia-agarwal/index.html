SoniaAgarwal

Actor (mainly Tamil cinema)

  • Born28 March 1982
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 4languages: Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada
  • 2004ITFA Best New Actress

Sonia Agarwal built her career in Tamil cinema, with work in Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada films too. Her breakthrough came with Kaadhal Kondein (2003), followed by 7G Rainbow Colony and Pudhupettai.

From Chandigarh to Chennai

Born in Chandigarh, she was offered a Zee TV serial while still at school, then debuted in film with a small role in the Telugu film Nee Premakai (2002) and the Kannada film Chandu.

Breakthrough

Her first Tamil film, Kaadhal Kondein with Dhanush, was a major success and won her the International Tamil Film Award for Best New Actress. 7G Rainbow Colony and the critically acclaimed Pudhupettai followed.

A long second innings

Returning to acting after 2010, she has continued in Tamil films and television, including the serial Malli and lead roles in films such as 7/G (2024).
