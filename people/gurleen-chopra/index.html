GurleenChopra

Actor and model

  • Born30 December 1990
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 6film languages
  • MissChandigarh title holder

Gurleen Chopra won the Miss Chandigarh title before moving into modelling and film. She has appeared in six film industries — Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Kannada and Punjabi.

Miss Chandigarh

Crowned Miss Chandigarh in her youth, she was encouraged by friends to model and debuted in Surjit Bindrakhiya’s music video “Tedi Tedi Takdi Tu”.

Six film industries

Her Hindi films include Indian Babu and Kuch To Gadbad Hai. In Telugu she debuted with Ayudham and drew praise for a double role in Manmatha. She later found success in Punjabi cinema with Hashar opposite Babbu Mann, and began her Marathi debut, Shinma, in 2015.
