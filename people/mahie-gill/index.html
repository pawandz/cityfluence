MahieGill

Actor (Hindi and Punjabi cinema)

  • Born19 December 1975
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 2010Filmfare Critics Award
  • 3debut honours for Dev.D (Filmfare, Screen, IIFA)

Mahie Gill began in Punjabi cinema and theatre before her breakthrough as Paro in Anurag Kashyap’s Dev.D (2009), a modern retelling of Devdas that won her the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress.

Theatre at Panjab University

Born Rimpy Kaur Gill in Chandigarh, she completed a master’s degree in theatre at Panjab University in 1998 and worked in theatre and Punjabi films before moving to Hindi cinema.

Breakthrough with Dev.D

Anurag Kashyap cast her as Paro in Dev.D, a role that brought critical acclaim and several debut awards. She went on to play Madhavi Devi in Saheb, Biwi Aur Gangster and its sequel, earning a Filmfare Best Actress nomination, and appeared in Paan Singh Tomar.

Streaming era

She has since worked in web series including 1962: The War in the Hills and the second season of Your Honor.
