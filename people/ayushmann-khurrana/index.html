Ayushmann Khurrana is known for playing ordinary men pushing against social norms. He has won a National Film Award and several Filmfare Awards, and Time named him one of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2020.

A Chandigarh theatre kid

Born Nishant Khurrana in Chandigarh, he studied at St. John’s High School and DAV College, then took a master’s in mass communication at Panjab University. He spent five years in theatre and was a founding member of DAV College’s “Aaghaaz” and “Manchtantra” groups, which are still active in the city.

Radio, Roadies and Vicky Donor

He worked in radio, won MTV Roadies season 2 in 2004 and hosted television before his film debut in Vicky Donor (2012), which earned him the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut — and a Best Male Playback Singer award for “Pani Da Rang”.

A string of hits

Films including Dum Laga Ke Haisha, Bareilly Ki Barfi, Badhaai Ho, Dream Girl and Bala made him a box-office regular. Andhadhun (2018) won him the National Film Award for Best Actor, and he became a UNICEF national Goodwill Ambassador in 2023.
