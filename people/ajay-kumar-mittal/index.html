AjayKumar Mittal

Former Chief Justice, Madhya Pradesh and Meghalaya High Courts

  • Born30 September 1958
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 2High Courts led as Chief Justice

Ajay Kumar Mittal is a retired judge who served as Chief Justice of the Meghalaya High Court and then the Madhya Pradesh High Court, after a long career at the Punjab and Haryana High Court in Chandigarh.

Born in Chandigarh

Justice Mittal was born in Chandigarh, studied commerce at Shri Ram College of Commerce and law at the University of Delhi, and began practising at the Punjab and Haryana High Court in 1980.

On the bench

He became a judge of that court in 2004 and served as its acting Chief Justice in 2018. He took oath as Chief Justice of the Meghalaya High Court in May 2019, moved to the Madhya Pradesh High Court that November, and retired in September 2020.
