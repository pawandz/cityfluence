BaniJ

Fitness model, actor and former MTV presenter

  • Born29 November 1987
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 2×reality-show runner-up (Roadies 4, Bigg Boss 10)

Gurbani Judge, known as Bani J, is a fitness model, actor and former MTV India presenter. She finished as runner-up on MTV Roadies 4 and Bigg Boss 10, and starred in the series Four More Shots Please!

From Chandigarh to MTV

Born in Chandigarh, Bani studied at Woodstock School, Mussoorie. She began her career on MTV Roadies 4, finishing first runner-up, and stayed with the show for Roadies 5.

Screens big and small

She competed in Fear Factor: Khatron Ke Khiladi 4, was runner-up on Bigg Boss 10 in 2016, appeared in music videos and films, and played Umang Singh in Four More Shots Please!
