SandeshJhingan

Footballer (centre-back, India)

  • Born21 July 1993
  • BirthplaceChandigarh, India
  • 1stIndian to play in Croatia
  • 2020Arjuna Award
  • 2020–21AIFF Men’s Player of the Year

Sandesh Jhingan is a centre-back for the India national team and FC Goa. He won the AIFF Emerging Player award in 2014, the Arjuna Award in 2020, and became the first Indian to play in the Croatian top flight.

Chandigarh academy days

Born in Chandigarh, he trained at St Stephen’s Academy, helped it reach the South-East Asian finals of the Manchester United Premier Cup, and won the B.C. Roy Trophy with the Chandigarh under-19 team — despite early rejections at trials in Kolkata.

Kerala Blasters

After starting at United Sikkim, he joined Kerala Blasters for the first Indian Super League in 2014, was named Emerging Player of the League, played in the final, and later captained the club.

Croatia and back

In 2021 he signed for HNK Šibenik, becoming the first Indian to play in Croatia. He has since played for ATK Mohun Bagan and Bengaluru FC, and joined FC Goa in 2023.
