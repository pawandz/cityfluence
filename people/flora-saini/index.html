Flora Saini — also credited as Asha Saini and Mayuri — has worked mainly in Telugu and Hindi films, with more than 80 acting projects and over 50 films since her 1999 debut.

An army childhood

Born in Chandigarh to an army officer, Flora went to school in Udhampur and at the Army Public School in Delhi before the family moved to Kolkata, where she began modelling and took part in the Miss Kolkata pageant.

Across South Indian cinema

She debuted in the Telugu film Prema Kosam (1999) and appeared in films including Nuvvu Naaku Nachav and Narasimha Naidu, as well as Kannada titles such as Nammanna. Her Hindi films include Love in Nepal (2004).

Web series

She later appeared in streaming series including the second season of Gandii Baat.
